Remove product photo backgrounds fast with AI

White or transparent backgrounds keep listings clean and consistent. You can remove image backgrounds online with AI — no Photoshop needed.

Sellers on Shopee, TikTok Shop, Amazon or Facebook often need to cut products out of living rooms, wooden tables or busy walls. The in-browser remove background tool gives you a PNG with an alpha channel in seconds.

Which photos work best?

  • Products on a fairly clean background with even lighting.
  • A subject that clearly stands out from the background (avoid very fine hair, fur or mesh if possible).
  • High enough resolution — blurry photos make sharp edges hard.
Remove backgrounds for free Add a JPG/PNG, preview it on a checkered background and download a transparent PNG. Open Remove Background →

Steps to cut out product photos

  1. Open OneTool Remove Background.
  2. Choose a product photo (JPG, PNG, WebP).
  3. Click remove background and preview it on a checkered backdrop.
  4. Download the transparent PNG, place it in Canva or on a white background, then post it to your store.

Once you have a transparent PNG

  • Put it on a white 1×1 background or follow your marketplace's guidelines.
  • Use Crop Image + Social Presets if you need feed / story ratios.
  • Reduce file size with Compress Image before bulk uploading.
  • iPhone HEIC photos? Convert them to JPG first with HEIC to JPG.

Frequently asked questions

Is background removal free?
Yes. OneTool lets you remove backgrounds, preview the result and download a transparent PNG without signing up.

Does it work for portraits?
Yes — it's great for ID photos and avatars; results are better with simple backgrounds.

What file do I get?
A PNG with a transparent background, ready for online stores, Canva or banners.
