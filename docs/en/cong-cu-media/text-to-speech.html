How to use Text to Speech

  1. Paste your text (up to 8,000 characters) or open a .txt file.
  2. Choose a male/female voice, style and speed.
  3. Click Preview to listen on your device, or Create audio file to download a WAV.

What is text to speech?

Text to speech (TTS) turns written text into a spoken voice. OneTool's voices are tuned for Vietnamese and also read English: pick a male or female voice, preview it and download a WAV for videos, vocabulary practice or voice-overs.

How to convert text to speech

Paste your text, choose a voice, then click Preview (runs on your device) or Create audio file to download it. Up to 8,000 characters at a time.

What to use it for

  • Learning — listen to lessons, practice pronunciation, read aloud for visually impaired users.
  • Content — voice-overs for TikTok, Reels and tutorials.
  • Work — listen to emails or meeting notes on the go.

Frequently asked questions

Is the text to speech tool free?
Yes. OneTool reads text aloud with natural voices, lets you preview it on your device and download a WAV file — no sign-up needed.
Is my text stored?
Preview runs in your browser. Creating a WAV file sends the text temporarily to a speech service — OneTool doesn't store your text or audio.
Can it read English?
Yes. Paste English or mixed Vietnamese–English text. The voices are optimized for Vietnamese.
