How to trim video online

  1. Drop a video (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV).
  2. Drag the start–end handles or enter seconds / mm:ss.
  3. Choose Fast copy or Precise encode; optionally mute.
  4. Click Trim video and download the result.

What is time-based video trimming?

Video Trim lets you pick a start–end range and export a new clip — no software to install. Everything runs in your browser; files are never uploaded.

Fast copy or precise encode?

  • Fast (stream copy) keeps the original codec, very fast; cut points may shift slightly to the nearest keyframe.
  • Precise (re-encode) libx264 + AAC, frame-accurate cuts, takes longer to process.

Tips

  • Set markers — play the video and click Set start / Set end at the current position.
  • Preview the range — use Play selection to check it before cutting.
  • Mute — tick Mute if you only need the picture without audio.
