What is AI summarization?

AI summarization condenses articles, meeting notes, long emails or study material into the key points. OneTool works in English and Vietnamese and outputs a paragraph, bullet points or a TL;DR + key points.

How to summarize text online

Paste your content into the source box (or open a file), choose the length and format, then click Summarize with AI. When it's done, copy the result or download a TXT/Markdown file.

When to use it

  • Quick reading — skim long articles or newsletters before reading in depth.
  • Meetings — pull action items from meeting notes.
  • Studying — summarize key concepts for revision.
