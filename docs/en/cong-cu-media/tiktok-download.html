How to use the TikTok Downloader

  1. Open TikTok → Share → Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into OneTool and click Get video.
  3. Choose HD MP4 · No watermark to download it.

What is a TikTok downloader without watermark?

TikTok Downloader (no watermark) gets the MP4 file from a public link for content you own or are allowed to use. You can also download MP3 or slideshow images; please respect copyright and TikTok's terms.

How to download TikTok videos without the watermark

Copy the video link → paste it into the Link box → click Get video → choose HD MP4 · No watermark. Supports tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com and vm.tiktok.com.

Things to know

  • Usage rights — only download content you have rights to (your own videos or with permission).
  • Private videos — private or expired links may not work.
  • Cloud processing — the tool sends the link to a service to fetch the file; videos are never stored long-term on OneTool.
