How to use Video to MP3

  1. Drop a video file (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM…).
  2. Click Extract MP3 — your browser keeps the audio and drops the picture.
  3. Preview, then download the 192 kbps MP3.

What is Video to MP3?

Video → MP3 pulls the audio track out of a clip into a 192 kbps MP3. Use it when you only need the music or dialogue, not the picture.

How to extract MP3 from video online

Drop an MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM and click Extract MP3. The first time, your browser downloads the engine (~25 MB); after that it's cached. Preview, then download the file.

When to extract MP3

  • Music / podcasts — get audio from videos saved on your device (when you have the rights).
  • Studying — replay lectures as audio only to save space.
  • Compress video — if you need to shrink the video itself, use Compress Video Online.

Frequently asked questions

Is converting video to MP3 free?
Yes. OneTool extracts MP3 from MP4/MOV/MKV right in your browser — no sign-up, no watermark.
Is my video uploaded to a server?
No. FFmpeg runs inside your browser — your video is never sent to OneTool's servers.
