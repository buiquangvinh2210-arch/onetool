How to use the SRT Subtitle Generator

          
  1. Drop an MP4/MOV video or an MP3/WAV file.
  2. Choose the language (or auto-detect), then click Create SRT subtitles.
  3. Download the .srt file and add it in CapCut, YouTube or Premiere.

        
What are SRT subtitles?
SRT is a subtitle file with numbered lines, timestamps and dialogue. OneTool turns video / MP3 into an SRT file you can add to videos for YouTube, TikTok (CapCut) or Premiere.
How to create SRT subtitles from a video
Drop an MP4, MOV or MP3, choose the language, and click Create SRT subtitles. When it's done, download the `.srt` — you can also copy the text or download a TXT version for editing.
What to do with SRT subtitles
  • YouTube / Shorts — upload the SRT in YouTube Studio to turn on CC.
  • CapCut / Premiere — drag the SRT onto the timeline and adjust lines to match the speech.
  • Study / meetings — review time-coded dialogue instead of replaying the whole clip.

        
Frequently asked questions

          
Is the online SRT generator free?
Yes. OneTool recognizes speech from video or MP3 and exports a time-coded SRT file — no sign-up needed.
Where do I add the SRT file?
Drag the `.srt` file into CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci Resolve, or upload it in YouTube Studio → Subtitles. Many video players also load an SRT with the same name as the MP4.
Are files stored on a server?
Files are processed temporarily in the cloud for speech recognition — no OneTool account needed.
