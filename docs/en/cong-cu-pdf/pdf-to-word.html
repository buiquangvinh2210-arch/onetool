What is PDF to Word?

      
PDF to Word (PDF to DOCX) converts a PDF into an editable document. OneTool supports both PDFs with a text layer and scanned documents (OCR).

      
Free, no sign-up required.

    

    

      
Frequently asked questions

      

        
Is PDF to Word free?
Yes. Converting PDF → DOCX on OneTool is free.

        
Can scanned PDFs be converted?
Yes — OCR is used for scans/images.

        
Will the layout look exactly like the PDF?
The DOCX prioritizes editable text; complex tables/images may need light touch-ups.
