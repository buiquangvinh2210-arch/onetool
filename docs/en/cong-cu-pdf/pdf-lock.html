How to password-protect a PDF

  1. Drop or choose an unlocked PDF.
  2. Enter an open password and confirm it.
  3. Click Lock PDF, then download the AES-256 encrypted file.

What does locking a PDF do?

Password-protecting a PDF encrypts the document with AES-256, so recipients must enter a password to view it — ideal for applications, contracts and financial reports.

How to send files safely

  • Separate password — send the password by chat or SMS, never in the same email as the file.
  • Strength — use ≥ 10 characters with upper case, lower case and numbers.
  • Already-locked PDFs — this tool can't open or change the password of an already-encrypted file.

Private, on your device

All encryption runs in your browser — your PDF is never uploaded to OneTool's servers.
