How to sign a PDF online

  1. Drop the PDF you want to sign.
  2. Create a signature: draw it, type your name, upload an image or pick a date.
  3. Click Place signature on PDF, then drag it into place.
  4. Click Download signed PDF.

What is online PDF signing?

Sign PDF adds a handwritten signature, your name, the signing date or a signature line to a document — contracts, minutes, delivery notes, job applications. The result is a PDF with the signature flattened into the page, so it opens on any device.

How to sign a PDF for free on OneTool

Drop a PDF and choose Draw, Type, Image or Date. Click Place signature on PDF, drag it into position, then Download signed PDF.

Which signature type should I use?

  • Draw — quick with a finger or mouse, just like signing on paper.
  • Type — pick a font like Pacifico or Great Vibes when you don't have a signature image.
  • PNG image — a scanned signature; the white background is made nearly transparent.
  • Date — stamp the signing date.

Save your signature on this device

Signatures you place are saved in your browser (never uploaded). Next time you open the tool, click a saved signature to place it again quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Is signing PDFs online free?
Yes. OneTool lets you sign PDFs for free: draw a handwritten signature, type your name in a signature font, upload an image or stamp the date — no sign-up needed.
Is this a certificate-based digital signature?
No — it's an image-based electronic signature embedded in the PDF: fine for internal contracts, minutes and delivery notes. It doesn't replace a certificate-based digital signature where the law requires one.
Is my PDF uploaded?
No. Everything runs in your browser — your PDF and signature are never sent to OneTool's servers.
Can I sign multiple pages?
Yes. Select the signature on the page and click Add to all pages.
