How to use Crop Image + Social Presets

  1. Upload a JPG / PNG / WebP image (or paste with Ctrl+V).
  2. Choose an aspect ratio or social preset (Instagram, TikTok, online store, ID photo…).
  3. Drag the crop frame, rotate/flip if needed, then click Download cropped image.

What is online image cropping?

Cropping selects an area of the original image and exports it at a new size — for avatars, Instagram feeds, vertical TikTok/Reels videos, product photos or 3×4 ID photos.

Built-in social media presets

  • Instagram & Reels square 1080×1080, portrait 4:5, Story/Reels 9:16.
  • TikTok & YouTube TikTok 1080×1920, thumbnail 1280×720, Shorts 9:16.
  • Facebook & online stores posts 1200×630, cover photos, 1:1 products, 3×4 ID photos.

How to crop images for social media

Upload an image, pick the preset for your platform, drag the frame (with a rule-of-thirds grid), choose to export the exact crop or scale to the preset, then download JPG / PNG / WebP.

Frequently asked questions

Is cropping images online free?
Yes. OneTool lets you crop images for free by aspect ratio or social media preset — no sign-up needed.
Are there Instagram, TikTok and store presets?
Yes. Pick from Instagram square/portrait/Story, TikTok 9:16, YouTube thumbnail, Facebook cover, 1:1 product photo, 3×4 ID photo and many more sizes.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. All cropping runs in your browser — files are never sent to OneTool's servers.
Can I crop a circle for an avatar?
Yes. Choose the Circle shape (locked to 1:1) and export as PNG or WebP to keep the transparent background; JPG will use a white background.
