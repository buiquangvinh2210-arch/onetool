Remove Background Online

Remove the background and export a transparent PNG — preview before downloading.

Drop an image here

or · JPG PNG WebP · up to 8 MB

How to use Remove Background

  1. Upload a portrait, product photo or logo (JPG, PNG, WebP).
  2. Click Remove background — the subject is cut out of the backdrop.
  3. Preview on a checkered background, then download the transparent PNG.

What is online background removal?

Background removal cuts a person, product or logo out of its backdrop and exports a transparent PNG — for online stores, Canva or ID photos.

Free, no sign-up required. Read the guide remove product photo backgrounds with AI.

Frequently asked questions

Is background removal free?
Yes. Preview and download a transparent PNG without signing up.
Which images work best?
Product photos on clean backgrounds, evenly lit portraits, or logos on plain backgrounds.
What file do I get?
A PNG with an alpha channel (transparent background).
