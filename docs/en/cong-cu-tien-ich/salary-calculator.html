How to use the Gross ↔ Net Salary Calculator

  1. Choose the direction: Gross → Net or Net → Gross.
  2. Enter the salary, number of dependants and the region where the company operates.
  3. See the take-home pay, income tax per bracket and total employer cost.

Net salary formula in 2026

Net salary = Gross salary − Mandatory insurance − Personal income tax.

  • Employee insurance 10.5%: social insurance 8%, health insurance 1.5%, unemployment insurance 1%. Social and health insurance are capped at 20× the reference level (50.6 million from 1 Jul 2026); unemployment insurance at 20× the regional minimum wage.
  • Family deductions: 15.5 million/month for yourself and 6.2 million/month per dependant (Resolution 110/2025/UBTVQH15, from the 2026 tax period).
  • Taxable income = Gross salary − Insurance − Deductions.

5-bracket income tax table from 2026

  • Bracket 1: up to 10 million/month — 5%.
  • Bracket 2: over 10 to 30 million — 10%.
  • Bracket 3: over 30 to 60 million — 20%.
  • Bracket 4: over 60 to 100 million — 30%.
  • Bracket 5: over 100 million — 35%.

The tax table follows Vietnam's 2025 Personal Income Tax Law; the salary and wage provisions apply from the 2026 tax period. See the guide to net salary in 2026, with examples and a comparison with 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What's the net pay on a 20 million VND gross salary in 2026?
With no dependants and insurance paid on the full 20 million: insurance 2.1 million, taxable income 2.4 million, income tax 120,000 VND — net salary about 17,780,000 VND.
At what salary do you start paying income tax?
For someone with no dependants who pays insurance on their full salary, tax only starts at about 17.3 million/month. Each dependant raises the threshold by 6.2 million.
Is the result 100% exact?
The tool follows the general rules for resident employees on contracts of 3 months or more. Tax-exempt allowances, other income or company-specific policies may change your actual take-home pay — check with your HR department.
