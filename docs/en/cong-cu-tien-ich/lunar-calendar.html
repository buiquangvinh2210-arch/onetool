        How to use the Lunar Calendar

        1. Open the Month tab — today is highlighted, and each cell shows both solar and lunar dates.
        2. Click a day to see its Stem–Branch, auspicious hours, day officer and do's / don'ts. Choose an activity under Find good days to highlight suitable days in green.
        3. Go to the Convert date tab to convert Solar ↔ Lunar for memorial days or lunar birthdays — leap months included.

        Lunar calendar today & perpetual calendar

        The lunar calendar page shows a monthly calendar based on the Vietnamese lunar calendar: each cell has the solar and lunar dates, with the 1st and 15th of each lunar month marked, plus holidays such as Lunar New Year (Tết), Hung Kings' Day, Vu Lan, Mid-Autumn Festival, 30/4 and 2/9.

        Click any day to see the Heavenly Stem–Earthly Branch (Can Chi) of the day / month / year, the solar term and auspicious hours. Switch to the Convert date tab to convert Solar ↔ Lunar for memorial days, weddings or lunar birthdays.

        Lunar calendar 2026 — Year of the Horse (Bính Ngọ)

        The lunar year Bính Ngọ starts on 17 Feb 2026, has 12 months and no leap month. Lunar New Year 2027 (Đinh Mùi) falls on Saturday 6 Feb 2027; the 12th month is short, so New Year's Eve is the 29th of the 12th month (5 Feb 2027).

        • 1st of the remaining lunar months: 10 Oct, 9 Nov, 9 Dec 2026 and 8 Jan 2027 (1st of the 12th month).
        • Full moons of the remaining months: 24 Oct, 23 Nov, 23 Dec 2026 and 22 Jan 2027.
        • Kitchen Gods' Day (23rd of the 12th month): Saturday 30 Jan 2027.

        Find good days by activity

        • Find good days by activity — pick weddings, store openings, groundbreaking, moving house, travel, buying a car or signing contracts; the calendar filters auspicious days and suitable day officers, avoiding Tam Nương, Nguyệt Kỵ and clashing days.
        • Do's and don'ts — each day has a day officer (Kiến, Trừ, Mãn…) with suggestions on what to do and avoid.

        How is the Vietnamese lunar calendar different?

        • UTC+7 — calculated for Vietnam (Hồ Ngọc Đức's algorithm), so the 1st and Tết may differ by one day from the Chinese calendar.
        • Leap month — some years have an extra lunar month; tick it correctly when converting Lunar → Solar.
        • Can Chi — a 60-year / 60-day cycle; years, months and days all have a Stem–Branch name.
        • Range — supports roughly 1800–2199. Results are for reference.

        Frequently asked questions

        What is today's lunar date?
        It's right there when you open the page: today is highlighted on the monthly calendar, and the side panel shows the lunar day, lunar month, the Stem–Branch year and how many days are left until the 1st, the full moon and Tết.
        When is Lunar New Year 2027?
        Lunar New Year (Đinh Mùi) is on Saturday 6 Feb 2027. The Bính Ngọ year has a short 12th month, so there's no 30th — New Year's Eve is the 29th of the 12th month (5 Feb 2027). The page has a countdown to Tết.
        Can I save memorial days by the lunar calendar?
        Yes. In My memorial days & events, enter a name and a lunar date (e.g. a memorial on the 25th of the 8th lunar month) — the calendar marks it every year, counts down the days left and has a button to add it to Google Calendar. Data is stored only in your browser.
        How are auspicious hours calculated?
        Based on the traditional method using the day's Earthly Branch: each day has 6 auspicious hours (each lasting 2 hours). This information reflects folk custom and is for reference only.
        Does lunar-to-solar conversion handle leap months?
        Yes. In the Convert date tab, choose Lunar → Solar and tick leap month if the date falls in a leap month (e.g. 2025 has a leap 6th month).
        Does this calendar use Vietnam's time zone?
        Yes. The calendar uses UTC+7 (Hồ Ngọc Đức's algorithm), so the 1st of each month and Tết match the Vietnamese calendar and may differ by one day from the Chinese calendar.
        How do I find good days for openings or weddings?
        In the Month tab, choose an activity under Find good days — suitable days are highlighted in green and listed below with their day officer and auspicious star. If you've entered your birth year, days that clash with it are excluded too. This follows folk custom and is for reference only.
