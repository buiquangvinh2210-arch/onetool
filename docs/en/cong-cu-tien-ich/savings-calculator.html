How to use the Savings Interest Calculator

  1. Choose a one-time deposit or monthly savings, then enter the amount and the rate in %/year.
  2. Choose the term and how interest is paid: at maturity, monthly or rolled over for several terms.
  3. See the interest, the total received and a table by month, term or year.

Savings interest formula

Interest = Deposit × Rate (%/year) × Months ÷ 12.

  • Interest at maturity: you receive principal and interest at the end. Deposit 100 million at 6%/year for 12 months and the interest is 100,000,000 × 6% × 12 ÷ 12 = 6,000,000 VND.
  • Monthly interest: each month you receive Deposit × Rate ÷ 12, and the principal stays the same. Banks often offer a slightly lower rate for monthly payouts than for payment at maturity.
  • Rollover of principal and interest: at the end of each term, interest is added to the principal and redeposited, so each term earns more than the last (compound interest).
  • Monthly savings: you add a fixed amount every month; interest is calculated monthly and added to the balance.

Frequently asked questions

How much does 100 million at 5%/year for 6 months earn?
Interest is 100,000,000 × 5% × 6 ÷ 12 = 2,500,000 VND. At maturity you receive 102,500,000 VND if interest is paid at the end.
How much monthly interest does 1 billion earn?
At 5%/year, monthly interest is about 1,000,000,000 × 5% ÷ 12 ≈ 4,166,667 VND. Higher rates increase the monthly interest proportionally.
Is rolling over principal and interest better than withdrawing interest?
Yes, if you don't need to spend the interest. Each term's interest is added to the principal, so the next term earns interest on a larger amount. The longer you save, the bigger the difference.
What if I withdraw early?
Most banks only pay the demand-deposit rate (usually very low) for the days deposited if you withdraw early. Each bank has its own rules, so ask before depositing.
