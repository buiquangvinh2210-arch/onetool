Vietnamese Number to Words

Spell out amounts in Vietnamese for invoices, contracts and payment orders — runs on your device; numbers are never sent to a server.

Enter a number to convert it to words.

How to use Number to Words

  1. Enter a number (dots as thousands separators are fine).
  2. Turn on “đồng chẵn” (even amount) for invoices.
  3. Copy the words into Word / PDF.

What is a Vietnamese number-to-words converter?

This tool spells out numbers (including money amounts) in Vietnamese words — used on invoices, contracts and payment orders to prevent tampering.

How to use

Type or paste a number, tick đồng chẵn (even amount) if needed, and copy the result. Processed in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Writing salaries in words in contracts

Vietnamese employment contracts usually state the gross salary in both figures and words. Not sure of your take-home pay? Use the Gross ↔ Net salary calculator 2026 to get the net amount after insurance and income tax, then spell it out here.

Frequently asked questions

How do you write 1,250,000 in Vietnamese words?
Một triệu hai trăm năm mươi nghìn đồng chẵn (one million two hundred fifty thousand dong) — exactly as written on Vietnamese invoices and contracts.
