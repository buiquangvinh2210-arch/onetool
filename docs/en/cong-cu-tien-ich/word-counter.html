Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the word count?
OneTool counts whitespace-separated tokens — accurate for English and Vietnamese (each syllable is one unit) — with an estimated reading time.
Does it count characters without spaces?
Yes — it shows words, characters (with/without spaces), sentences and paragraphs for SEO articles or essays.
