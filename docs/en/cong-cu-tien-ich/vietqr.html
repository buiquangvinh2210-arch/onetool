How to use the VietQR Generator

  1. Choose the receiving bank (NAPAS BIN code).
  2. Enter the account number, plus an optional amount and transfer note (no accents).
  3. Create the code, test-scan it with a banking app, then download the PNG.

What is VietQR?

VietQR is Vietnam's interbank transfer QR code. Customers scan it with a banking app (MB, Vietcombank, MoMo…) and get the right account number without typing.

How to create a VietQR code for free

Choose the bank, enter the account number, click Create VietQR, test-scan it once, then print or share the image. Amount and transfer note are optional.

Frequently asked questions

How is VietQR different from a regular QR code?
VietQR follows the NAPAS standard: banking apps read the bank BIN + account number + amount. A plain text QR only opens a web link.
Do I have to enter an amount?
No. Leave it empty and the payer enters the amount in their app. Fill it in and the exact amount appears after scanning.
