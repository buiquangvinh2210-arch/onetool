How to use the Currency Converter

  1. Choose the source and target currencies (USD → VND by default).
  2. Enter an amount — the result and exchange rate appear instantly.
  3. Click swap or a quick pair; copy the result if needed.

What is an online currency converter?

Currency Converter converts between currencies (USD, EUR, VND…) using daily-updated reference rates — handy for estimating international purchases, travel and transfers.

Popular pairs

  • USD → VND — estimate US / USD prices.
  • EUR → VND — travel in Europe, EU goods.
  • JPY / CNY / KRW — shopping from Japan · China · Korea.

A note on rates

These are reference rates from public sources, not a bank's buy/sell rates. For real transactions, check with your bank.
