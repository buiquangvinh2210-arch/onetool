Lump-sum social insurance formula

Lump sum = 1.5 × Mbq × years paid before 2014 + 2 × Mbq × years paid from 2014. Mbq is the average monthly insured salary.

  • Rounding leftover months: 1 to 6 months counts as half a year; 7 to 11 months counts as a full year.
  • Leftover months before 2014 are carried into the from-2014 period, so they earn the 2-month rate.
  • Less than one year: you receive what was contributed (22% of the monthly insured salary × number of months), up to 2 months of average salary.
  • Average salary is the average monthly insured salary over your whole history, after applying each year's inflation adjustment factor. People on the State salary scale have a separate averaging method based on their final years.

Who can withdraw social insurance as a lump sum?

Under Vietnam's 2024 Social Insurance Law (effective 1 July 2025), common cases include:

  • Reaching retirement age with fewer than 15 years of contributions and not continuing to contribute.
  • Emigrating abroad permanently.
  • Having one of the life-threatening illnesses listed in the law (such as cancer, polio or decompensated cirrhosis).
  • People who started contributing before 1 July 2025, have not paid compulsory or voluntary social insurance for 12 months, and have fewer than 20 years of contributions.

Withdrawing a lump sum erases your contribution history, so it no longer counts toward a future pension. Think carefully or ask your local social insurance office before applying.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the lump sum after 5 years of contributions?
If all 5 years were paid from 2014 onward with an average salary of 8 million VND: 2 × 8,000,000 × 5 = 80,000,000 VND, before inflation adjustment.
How many years is 5 years and 5 months?
5 leftover months falls in the 1–6 month range, so it counts as half a year — 5.5 years. 7 to 11 leftover months rounds up to a full year.
Can I withdraw with less than 1 year of contributions?
Yes, if you're eligible. The amount is 22% of the insured salary × months paid, up to 2 months of average salary. For example, 8 months at a 6 million salary: 22% × 6,000,000 × 8 = 10,560,000 VND.
What is the inflation adjustment factor and where do I find it?
It's a factor that adjusts past insured salaries for inflation, published every year for each contribution year. The older the contribution year, the larger the factor. Ask the social insurance agency or check the latest official notice; if left empty, the tool uses 1.
