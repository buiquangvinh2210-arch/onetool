How to use the Unit Converter

  1. Choose a unit group: weight, length, temperature, data size…
  2. Enter a number and choose the source unit — results appear as you type.
  3. Change the target unit or see the conversion to every unit in the group.

What is unit conversion?

Unit conversion converts a quantity between the metric (SI) system and the imperial/US customary system you see on imported goods, machine specs and recipes. OneTool gives each group its own page and uses international standard factors, so results match technical documentation.

Most common conversions

  • Weight: 1 kg = 2.2046 lb · 1 lb = 453.59 g · 1 oz = 28.35 g — see Weight Converter.
  • Length: 1 inch = 2.54 cm · 1 ft = 30.48 cm · 1 mile = 1.609 km — see Length Converter.
  • Temperature: °F = °C × 1.8 + 32 · 37 °C = 98.6 °F — see Temperature Converter.
  • Data size: 1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes, while 1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes — see Data Size Converter.
  • Area: 1 ha = 10,000 m² · 1 acre ≈ 4,047 m² — see Area Converter.
  • Pressure: 1 bar ≈ 14.5 psi · 1 atm = 101,325 Pa — see Pressure Converter.

Conversion factors used

Each unit is converted to a base unit (kg, m, m², liter, second, byte, joule, pascal, watt…) and then to the target unit, so converting back and forth never drifts. Factors follow the official definitions — for example, 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg and 1 inch = 0.0254 m are exact values under the 1959 international agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Which unit groups are available?
13 groups: weight, length, area, volume, temperature, speed, time, data size, energy, pressure, power, angle and fuel economy.
Are the results accurate?
Calculations use standard SI/NIST factors with floating-point precision (about 15 digits). The display is rounded for readability; click copy to get the full number.
